🧬 How to Play

Every living cube follows simple rules: it survives, is born, or dies based on how many neighbours it has. Paint a few cubes, press play, and watch a universe evolve.

🖱️ Mouse

  • Left-drag on world — use tool (paint / erase / bomb…)
  • Left-drag on space — orbit camera
  • Right / middle-drag — pan camera
  • Scroll — zoom  •  Shift+scroll — brush size
  • Alt+drag — always orbit

📱 Touch

  • 1 finger — paint / orbit  •  2 fingers — orbit + pinch zoom

⌨️ Keyboard

  • Space play/pause  •  . step  •  C clear  •  R random
  • 1–7 tools  •  [ ] brush size  •  + − speed
  • WASD / arrows pan  •  Q / E rotate  •  F focus
  • M mute  •  H help  •  Esc close

🎯 Tips

  • Earn 🧬 DNA from births & quests to level up and unlock tools.
  • Try Stamp → Glider Gun for endless spaceships.
  • Switch rules to change how life behaves — each world feels different!
  • Use 🎯 Focus to fly to where life is densest.
